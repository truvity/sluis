DEPRECATED: this release is configured through the `config` and `policy` values
(and `exchange`, `githubApps.catalogue`, `slackApps`), which the chart renders
into the service document and the policy document. That mode is removed after
the next minor. Render the two documents with `sluisctl render` from an
installation, and pass them as they are:

  --set-file documents.service=rendered/sluis.yaml
  --set-file documents.policy=rendered/policy.yaml

The chart then keeps only the deployment-level values (replicas, service
account, mounts, probes, routes, alerts) and does not rewrite the documents.
See docs/reference/sluisctl.md (render) and docs/decisions/0038-estates-render-through-sluis.md.
