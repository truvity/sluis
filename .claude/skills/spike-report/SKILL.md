---
name: spike-report
description: Use when running a spike, measuring something to answer a design question, or writing up a spike or research result for an issue, a pull request or a page.
---

Read "Spikes and research" in `docs/contributing/decisions.md` and follow it, with the writing style in `docs/contributing/pages.md` for the prose and `docs/contributing/public-content.md` for what may be published.

Checklist:

- [ ] The result has every part the page lists, in its order, verdict first.
- [ ] Every measurement is dated in UTC and names its versions.
- [ ] Every change made to any machine or device is listed.
- [ ] Open questions are options with pros, cons and a recommendation; nothing is decided for the maintainer.
- [ ] Nothing private or secret is in the text.
