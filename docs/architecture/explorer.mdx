This is the target state, not a running system. The map is drawn from `site/src/data/explorer/architecture.yaml`, written by hand from the design until the [permission manifest](/decisions/0002-the-design-model-actors-ports-drivers-and-contracts/#the-permission-manifest) exists and generates it. What each actor and driver is and does is on [Components](/architecture/components/); the words are on [Actors](/concepts/actors/) and [Ports and drivers](/concepts/drivers/).

The map draws three of the four [tested installations](/runtimes/installations/): local, boards and lambda. It does not draw the [Kubernetes installation](/runtimes/kubernetes/) yet. Pick an installation, and the map, the tables and the flows follow it. Without script the page shows boards, and every table is plain HTML.

## The map

Select a box to see what it holds, its manifest summary and its listeners; the lines show who it calls and who calls it. Choose a flow to step through it. A chip says whether a point is decided or proposed, and a proposed point links its tracking issue.
